About Us

More love. Less loneliness. For pets and people.

Our Mission

At Stay With My Pet, our mission is simple yet deeply meaningful — to create a world where pets receive more care, and people feel less alone.

We believe that every pet deserves love, attention, and joyful moments — even when their owners are busy, travelling, or facing life’s challenges. Our platform connects Pet Parents, who need trusted care for their beloved companions, with Pet Friends, who long for the warmth and joy that only animals can bring.
By building a safe, flexible, and compassionate pet-sharing community, we help pets live happier lives and bring people closer together — one tail wag, one purr, one smile at a time.

Pet Comes First Care, comfort, and wellbeing always lead the way.
Community over numbers Built on trust, respect, and real connection.

Our Story

Like many great ideas, Stay With My Pet was born from a personal dilemma.

We’re proud dog parents who absolutely adore our furry friend — but as life got busier and travelling became part of our routine, we often found ourselves needing someone kind and trustworthy to care for him.
There was another challenge too — we both have pet allergies. When we first adopted our dog, we didn’t realise how strong the reaction would be: itchy eyes, a runny nose, and skin irritation. Still, our love for him never faded. We simply had to find new ways to give him the care and attention he deserves — and we’re always grateful when someone visits, takes him for a walk, or gives him extra playtime. He’s full of energy and joy, and he deserves nothing less.
As we shared our experience with friends, colleagues, and family, we realised we weren’t alone. So many people face similar situations — pet parents who need temporary help or a break, and others who would love to spend time with animals but can’t have one full-time due to allergies, housing rules, or busy schedules.

That’s when we asked ourselves: why not bring these people together? That question grew into our mission — and Stay With My Pet was born.

Our Core Values

At Stay With My Pet, our core values guide every decision and every connection within our community. Everything begins with love and compassion — for pets, for people, and for the bonds between them. We prioritise safety, transparency, and responsible care, because every profile and every trusted connection matters. We believe in the power of community — in building friendships, supporting one another, and showing respect for both animals’ needs and people’s boundaries. And at the heart of it all is joy and wellbeing — because a shared moment with a pet can brighten a day, strengthen connections, and bring more warmth into life.
